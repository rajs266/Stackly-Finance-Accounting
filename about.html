Our Story

Building Trust Through Financial Excellence

Founded in 2011 in Salem, Tamil Nadu, Stackly began with a simple mission: make professional financial services accessible to every business, big or small. What started as a small CA firm has grown into a trusted partner for 1,200+ businesses across India.

Our team of 15+ Chartered Accountants, tax experts, and financial advisors work tirelessly to ensure your business stays compliant, optimised, and ready for growth. We believe in transparency, accuracy, and building long-term relationships.

    Timeline

    Our Journey of Growth & Success

    2011

    Founded in Salem

    Started as a small CA consultancy with just 2 partners, serving local textile businesses in Salem district.

    2014

    Expanded to 50+ Clients

    Hired our first team of 5 CAs and introduced GST readiness services ahead of the 2017 rollout.

    2017

    GST Transformation

    Became one of the first firms in Tamil Nadu to offer complete GST migration and compliance services.

    2020

    Digital-First Approach

    Launched cloud-based accounting and remote consultation services during the pandemic to serve clients nationwide.

    2023

    1,000+ Clients Milestone

    Crossed 1,000 active clients across 12 states. Introduced AI-powered financial analytics and advisory.

    2026

    Leading Financial Partner

    Now serving 1,200+ businesses with 15+ expert CAs, recognised as a top CA firm in Tamil Nadu.
